# Evidence Rules

Evidence records connect manuscript claims to source anchors and reviewer
judgments.

## Minimum Fields

- claim id;
- claim text;
- source unit;
- evidence anchor;
- evidence strength;
- uncertainty;
- reviewer notes.

## Strength Labels

- strong: directly supported by methods, data, or cited source;
- moderate: plausible but incomplete;
- weak: asserted with limited support;
- unsupported: no adequate support found;
- contradicted: evidence points against the claim.

## Rule

Do not collapse unsupported claims into writing-quality feedback. A claim can be
well written and still weakly evidenced.
