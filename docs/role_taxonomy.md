# Role Taxonomy

## Human Editor

Owns scope, judgment, accountability, and final decision.

## Primary Reviewer

Produces the main review: contribution, method, evidence, clarity, and revision
requirements.

## Verifier

Checks whether claims, calculations, citations, methods, and reported evidence
support the review conclusions.

## Dissent Reviewer

Argues against the current consensus and tests whether the review is overconfident.

## Evidence Clerk

Maintains the claim/evidence matrix and source anchors.

## Synthesis Writer

Combines reviews into a decision-ready brief while preserving unresolved
disagreements.
