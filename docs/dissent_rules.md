# Dissent Rules

Dissent is a first-class review artifact.

## Purpose

The dissent role exists to prevent premature consensus, author sympathy,
reviewer anchoring, and AI sycophancy.

## Requirements

A dissent review should:

- challenge the strongest emerging conclusion;
- name at least one plausible alternative interpretation;
- identify missing evidence that would change the decision;
- distinguish fatal flaws from repairable issues;
- avoid rhetorical aggression.

## Resolution

Synthesis may reject dissent, but it must record why. Do not erase dissent by
rewriting it into neutral summary language.
