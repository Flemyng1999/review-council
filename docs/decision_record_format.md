# Decision Record Format

Decision records belong under:

```text
cases/<case-id>/decision/
```

## Required Sections

- decision owner;
- manuscript identity;
- decision summary;
- major reasons;
- adopted reviewer critiques;
- rejected reviewer critiques;
- unresolved questions;
- required revisions;
- confidence and limits.

## Rule

The decision record should make it possible to distinguish:

- what AI reviewers said;
- what the human editor accepted;
- what the human editor rejected;
- what remains uncertain.
